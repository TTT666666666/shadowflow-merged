目的：**在 7 天内验证「listing 合规检查 + 改写」这个服务有没有人付钱。**
工具是后台，不上前台。买家看到的只有「一个人 + 一份报告」。

---

## 1. Gig 标题（80 字符上限，直接复制）

```
I will audit your Amazon listing for banned claims and rewrite it
```

备选（跑 7 天没曝光就换）：

```
I will fix your Amazon listing compliance risks and rewrite the copy
```

- 搜索标签：`amazon listing`、`listing optimization`、`product description`、`amazon compliance`、`amazon seo`

## 4. Gig 描述（前三行是搜索摘要，不要改顺序）

```
Your listing gets suppressed, not because the product is bad, but because one
sentence broke a rule nobody told you about. "FDA approved", "100% effective",
"best selling" — each of these can take a listing down overnight.

I check your copy against the actual Amazon and TikTok Shop policy word lists,
then rewrite it so the claim survives review.

WHAT YOU GET
- A PDF report scoring your listing on 6 dimensions (title, bullets, trust
  signals, price anchor, conversion path, compliance)
- Every problem quoted from your own copy, with the exact fix
- Rewritten title and bullets in native English (Standard and Premium)
- A list of certifications you would need if you want to keep a claim

HOW IT WORKS
The checks run on a rules engine, not a chatbot, so the same copy always gets
the same verdict — you can hand the report to a client or a lawyer and it will
not change next week.

WHAT I DO NOT DO
I do not guarantee your listing will stay up. Nobody can. I tell you what is
risky and how to say it safely, and you decide.

Send me your ASIN or paste your copy. You will have the report within 24 hours.
```

## 5. FAQ（至少填 4 条，影响转化）

```
Q: Which marketplaces do you cover?
A: Amazon (US / UK / DE listings in English) and TikTok Shop. If you sell on
   Shopify or Etsy, message me first — the rule set is different.

Q: Do you rewrite the copy or only flag problems?
A: Basic flags problems only. Standard and Premium include a full rewrite of
   the title and bullet points.

Q: Can you guarantee my listing won't be suppressed?
A: No, and be careful with anyone who says yes. I check your copy against
   published policy word lists and known enforcement patterns. That removes
   most risk, not all of it.

Q: What if I disagree with a flag?
A: Tell me and I will explain the rule it came from. If I am wrong, I remove
   it and revise for free.

Q: Do you need my Amazon account?
A: No. An ASIN or the copy pasted into a message is enough.
```

## 6. 买家需求表单（Requirements）

1. ASIN 或直接粘贴 listing 原文（标题 + 五点 + 描述）
2. 目标市场（US / UK / DE）
3. 类目（美妆、食品、母婴、器械、其他）
4. 有没有已通过的认证（FDA、CE、有机、检测报告）—— 有就发编号，我据此判断哪些宣称能保留

## 7. 交付流程（每单 20 分钟）

1. 复制买家文案 → 打开 `http://localhost:8443/#/app` → 商品诊断
2. 选平台（Amazon）+ 类目 → 诊断
3. 在「署名」栏填 `YourName · Fiverr` → 点「下载报告」
4. 打开下载的 HTML → 点右下角「存为 PDF」→ 得到 PDF
5. 人工过一遍改写建议（**没有模型 key 也能做，就是慢 10 分钟**）
6. Fiverr 里回复 + 附 PDF

## 8. 上架后 24 小时

- 头像用真人照片，简介写一句「I audit Amazon listings against policy word lists」
- 每天至少打开 Fiverr 两次，**响应时间 < 1 小时**（直接影响排名）
- 前 5 单可以主动降价到 $10，但**不要免费**——免费来的单不会给五星
- 同时挂一个英文的个人简介页（Fiverr 允许放一个外链）
